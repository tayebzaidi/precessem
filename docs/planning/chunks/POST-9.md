# POST-9 — Adjoint sensitivities of safety functionals

 **Adjoint sensitivities of safety functionals** — the gradient of a real functional `J(E)` (phantom absorbed power, accepted power, a region's averaged SAR, later a lead-tip SAR / temperature) with respect to many parameters at the cost of one extra back-substitution (operator directive 2026-09-28, interactive session; **Phase 9**, motivated by the worst-case implant-heating research direction: gradient-based search over lead trajectory / tissue properties / coil state instead of brute-force sampling, and first-order uncertainty on a safety margin). **Why it is cheap here:** the time-harmonic system `A(p) u = b` is complex-symmetric (`Aᵀ = A`, reciprocal media), so the adjoint `Aᴴ λ = (∂J/∂u)ᴴ` is `A λ̄ = conj(∂J/∂u)ᴴ` — the **same factorization** the forward solve already holds (`PORT-19` keeps one per sweep); then `dJ/dp_i = 2 Re(λᵀ (∂b/∂p_i − ∂A/∂p_i u)) + ∂J/∂p_i` for every `p_i` from assembled `∂A/∂p_i` forms, no extra solve per parameter. Theory: Jin 3e (FEM formulation and reciprocity chapters) for the discrete system; cite chapter/equation in step 0. **Steps (written now, placed by the weekly):** **0** — design note: functionals (smooth only — a 10 g-averaged SAR at a *fixed* cube, never a max, whose derivative is not defined at ties), parameter classes (per-region σ / εᵣ; per-cell σ as a field; lumped `C` and port drives through `PORT-15` / `POST-6`, where the gradient is exact circuit algebra; geometry / lead path later, shape derivative, a separate step), the conjugation convention written out once and tested. **1** — per-region material gradients, **gated on a closed form**: `dP_abs/dσ` for the `TH-1` lossy plane-wave slab and for the `TH-10` lossy sphere on an imposed field, each by differentiating the closed form (analytic, or complex-step on the series), at the imported `TH-1` / `TH-10` bands' order; plus a **Taylor test** (remainder `\|J(p + hδ) − J(p) − h ∇J·δ\|` falls as `h²`, observed rate ≥ 1.9 over ≥ 3 halvings) and a **negative control** (a deliberately un-conjugated adjoint fails the Taylor test at rate ≈ 1). **2** — the same on the F-small four-port birdcage (Cu, `TH-14`): gradients of phantom absorbed power w.r.t. phantom σ / εᵣ and coil σ, Taylor test, and a measured **cost ratio** (adjoint + all gradients vs one forward solve, asserted ≤ 1.5 on the held factorization). **3** — per-cell σ gradient on F-small, and first-order uncertainty `Var J ≈ ∇Jᵀ Σ ∇J` for a stated tissue-property covariance, checked against a small Monte-Carlo ensemble (a record, not a gate, until the ensemble size is justified). **4** — consumers, each its own chunk when opened: lead-path shape sensitivity for Phase 7 (`WF-9`), temperature functionals through the Phase 8 Pennes solve (coupled adjoint), worst-case search. **Serial on:** `TH-1` ✅, `TH-10` ✅, `TH-14` ✅, `PORT-19` ✅;
