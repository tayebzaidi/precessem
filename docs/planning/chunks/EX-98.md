**The wall on this fixture is a coefficient, not geometry. Read this first.**
- `MAT-15` P1's thermal domain is the tag-3 submesh of the EM mesh, and it has no wall cells. Step 3 therefore enters the wall as its series thermal resistance in the Robin coefficient: h_eff = 1 / (1/h + t_w/k₂), planar, with one scalar on the lateral faces and the caps.
