The script solves `PORT-1`'s two tori (tag 1 driven, 10 MHz, degree 1) in an air box whose outer wall (facet tag 1) is PEC, natural, or the first-order ABC (Jin 3e §9.2 (9.44), s = 1, `fem_em_solver.core.boundary_terms.first_order_abc_term`, through `TimeHarmonicSolver.solve(extra_bilinear_terms=…)`). It shows three things:
- **The ABC is in the matrix and it absorbs.** Re Z₁₁^ABC > 0 at every padding, and Re Z₁₁·I₁² equals ∮\|n × E\|²/η₀ over the wall. The PEC wall carries no tangential E (∮ 4.17e-37 W) and no Re Z. The natural wall carries a large tangential E (0.258 W of ∮\|n × E\|²/η₀) and still has Re Z₁₁ = 0: tangential E on the wall is not absorption.
- **At k₀·half-width ≈ 0.02–0.04 the ABC is a reflector.** It acts as the natural (magnetic) wall: its box term B^ABC = d^ABC − D∞^PEC is positive where PEC's is negative, +4.7133 / +2.3965 / +1.1256 pp at W = 0.08 / 0.10 / 0.12 m. Its deficit differs from the natural wall's by 0.224 pp at 0.08 m. Padding still matters under an ABC on this box.
- **The ⅓ PEC + ⅔ natural post-solve mix cancels the box term** to −0.2520 pp against \|B^PEC\|/5 = 1.9468 pp, and swapped weights miss by 2.5647×. It is computed from the same window's PEC and natural solves. It is a combination of two solves, not a boundary condition.

r(W) = \|B^ABC\|/\|B^PEC\| = 0.4846 / 0.3567 / 0.2266 is printed as a record beside the sphere model's 0.5. It is never asserted: (R) was retired by the 2026-10-07 weekly.
