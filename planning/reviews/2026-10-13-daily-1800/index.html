    Ruling 1 — item 424's park is a property of the fixture, not a defect; P26 re-posed as item 439 (provisional, attempt 2 of 4)¶

    The arithmetic. (FEM) as posed is max|a − b| / max|a|, where: - a = running_average(duty series); - b = Σ(−1)^m running_average(H_m series).

    running_average is linear, so a − b = running_average(r), where r is the per-step Duhamel residual that (H0) gates at L_BAND. A window mean cannot exceed the maximum of what it averages. So (FEM) inherits (H0)'s residual: |a − b| ≤ max|r|.

    Why this box differs from MAT-20. On this box the ten H_m means reach ≈ 1.2 K against a ≈ 0.6 K duty, so (H0) reads 3.799e-13 (20261013T183523Z_MAT-21-step2-W2-identities.log on the branch). On MAT-20's fixture (H0) read 5.689e-14, which is why the same assertion was green there. In absolute terms, (FEM) is 1.464e-13 × 0.61668 K = 9.03e-14 K, against max|r| = 3.799e-13 × 0.624543 K = 2.373e-13 K (:117–124). The ceiling is therefore ≈ 3.85e-13, which is above D_BAND. As posed, (FEM) at D_BAND is therefore not attainable on this fixture by construction. It is not an operator or solver defect.

    What did not change. D_BAND is not widened, and no band moves.

    Re-pose. This is a measurement that separates two readings (rule (6)); P26's target is unchanged. - (FEM′): the operator identity with the residual carried through it, asserted at D_BAND. - (FEM-c): the window-mean bound, asserted. - (FEM) as posed: printed beside its ceiling max|r|/max|a|. - W3: the physics gate ((T-duty) against the ten-run closed form) runs this time.
