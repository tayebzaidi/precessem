    Item 84 — TH-16 step 3b: the ✅ gate becomes an identity on a mirror-built mesh¶

    What was measured (20260930T003844Z_TH-16-step3b-W1.log:908–952). Everything that lives on the quarter mesh alone is green: premise, S⁺⁺ reciprocity 2.016e-14, passivity, and the memory pin (ΔM_q 0.370 GiB against 0.425). The class anchor against S_10MHZ_EPS0_RECORD is red by 1–4 % (s 3.682e-2, a 1.081e-2, o 1.024e-2 against 1e-3).

    The reading. The record comes from an independently meshed full birdcage. The two meshes differ where this fixture is most sensitive: - the quarter is 36 034 cells, so four of them are 144 136 against the record mesh's 116 085 (the cut faces conform the grading at the planes, which is where the sheets lie); - the narrowed sheets measure 7.419 / 7.488 mm against 7.294 mm.

    The (+,+) eigenvalue s + o + 2a, which reflects almost fully (0.999904 − 0.013259j), matches the record to 4e-5. The other two modes are both ≈ 2.3 % off — including s + o − 2a, which comes from the same (+,+) solve as the mode that matches. So the miss is consistent with the feed neighbourhood and not with the PEC cut: a wrong boundary condition would be unlikely to leave one eigenvalue of a solve at 4e-5 and move the other by 2 %. That is a reading, not an attribution; the mirror-built identity below is what tests it. This project already knows its degree-1 self class at 10 MHz is unconverged in the port neighbourhood (§2.2; the ANS-4 two-rung move is 4.4 %). The 09-29 18:00 band was "reachable, not guaranteed" on the evidence of a same-sizing remesh (8.1e-5); a quarter cut is not a same-sizing remesh. That prediction was this review's and it was wrong.

    The ruling. A two-mesh comparison at 1e-3 was never a test of what TH-16 claims — that PEC / PMC cut planes with the port rescaling reconstruct the full problem. That claim has an exact discrete form: on a full mesh built by mirroring the quarter, the quarter's three solves reconstruct the full 4×4 to solver precision (step 2 read ≤ 1.3e-15 on the box, band 1e-9). The ✅ gate is re-anchored there. This is not a loosening: - the 1e-3 two-mesh band is withdrawn as a gate, not widened, and the new band is six orders tighter on the quantity the chunk owns; - the two-mesh figures stay in the row as a record, and the attempt-2 window prints them again beside the identity; - the memory pin (≤ 0.425 GiB) and the one-mesh identities are kept and re-asserted.
