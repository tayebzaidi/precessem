    PORT-16 — The ≈1 %-of-supplied accounting gap on the 4-leg fixture¶

    The ≈1 %-of-supplied accounting gap on the 4-leg fixture — every single drive on the loaded F-small birdcage reads P_acc − ½∫σ\|E\|² ≈ 6.7e-05 W, the same absolute figure on all four ports (0.23 % scatter, POST-6 step 1b) and 0.98 % of supplied power, i.e. sitting at 0.98× the imported 1e-2 POWER_BALANCE_BAND. Diagnosis chunk (opened 2026-09-06 weekly review; serial on nothing): attribute the gap to one term by measurement — candidates, each a separate in-run print: (a) the lumped sheet's own dissipation ½ Re(Z_p)∫\|K\|² dA versus the volume loss the sheet's box carries; (b) the conductor/phantom partition of ∫σ\|E\|² against the sheet-facet flux; (c) h: the same three prints on WF-6 step 3f's finer phantom mesh and on PORT-14 step 1b's ×0.75 / ×0.6 conductor rungs, so the gap's exponent in h is measured, not guessed. Done-when: one candidate reproduces the gap on the record mesh to ≤ 10 % of itself with the other two printed and separated, a negative control (the sheet resistivity ×2 must move (a) and not (b)), and the gap's h-rate printed — then the review decides whether POWER_BALANCE_BAND is a band or a record. No band moves in this chunk.
