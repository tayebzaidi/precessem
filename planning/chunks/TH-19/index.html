    TH-19 — Carry the matched source projection to the coil, and retest the production element order¶

    Carry the matched source projection to the coil, and retest the production element order — opened 2026-09-10, operator direction. The decision this reopens. §10's TH-12 clause (2026-08-23) pins degree 1 for every coil-fed Phase-5/6 solve. It was never an accuracy judgement — degree 2 wins outright on the sphere, 0.1405 % against 3.643 % at 3.01× fewer cells — it was an identity failure: on the coil the complex-power identity Im Z = 4ω(W_m − W_e)/I′² read 4.5931e-09 loaded / 3.0030e-09 free against a 1e-9 family bound, while the degree-1 rows of the same run, same mesh, same process sat at 8.07e-15. The known-issues entry names the mechanism: W_m is unmoved (3.04e-08 → 3.13e-08 J) while W_e explodes 3.5e7× (2.03e-13 → 7.16e-06 J), so Im Z goes from the physical +9.02 Ω to −2 117 Ω. The ungauged curl-curl null space is far richer at second order (882 296 vs 162 710 dofs) and the penalty-free formulation lets irrotational content sit in E. The clause is explicitly reversible on evidence, and its condition is already satisfied. TH-13 closed the discriminator: the injector is the degree-1-only H¹₀ source projection — a plumbing defect in the excitation, not a property of the element. TH-13 step 3a then built the fix and measured it on a loop fixture: residue 8.1e-17 / 1.8e-16 against ≤ 1e-8, and the gradient share of W_e 4.6e-23 / 3.1e-21 against ≤ 1e-6 — six to fourteen orders of margin, with the default path bit-identical on its control. It landed as project_source="matched" (core/time_harmonic.py:50, which sets the projection degree to the solve's own and pins the exterior to the solve's own Dirichlet set) and has zero callers in tests/ or examples/. So the blocker is unqueued work, not an open question. What is NOT evidence, stated so a later reader does not mistake it: ANS-4 steps 2b and 2d ran fifteen degree-2 coil solves on 2026-09-09/10 and passed reciprocity, σ_max and the C4 spreads — but those are the weak gates (§2.2), and that module asserts no power identity. Nothing since August has tested the actual objection. One genuinely new datum does favour degree 2: on the identical mesh it gave tighter C4 spreads than degree 1 (0.0191 / 0.0334 / 0.0241 % against 0.1012 / 0.0916 / 0.0654 %), i.e. it is less sensitive to the sheet-triangulation asymmetry GEO-31 identified.
