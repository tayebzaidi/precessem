    Operator. cem43(times, temps, *, r_above=0.5, r_below=0.25, t_ref=43.0) takes a 1-D series, or a (n_t, n_cells) array (the "one DG0 series": one value per cell per time). Between samples it treats T as piecewise linear in t and integrates R^(t_ref − T) exactly on each segment: - ∫ R^(43−T) dt = (R^(43−T₀) − R^(43−T₁)) / (b ln R), with b = (T₁ − T₀)/Δt. - A segment that crosses 43 °C is split at t₍₄₃₎ = t₀ + (43 − T₀)/b. R = 0.5 applies on the side T ≥ 43 and R = 0.25 on the side T < 43. The integrand is continuous at 43, where both sides equal 1. - The b → 0 limit uses the expm1 form, Δt·R^(43−T₀)·(−expm1(−ΔT ln R))/(ΔT ln R), never the raw difference quotient, which cancels catastrophically when ΔT ≈ 0. - The §7 row's Σ R^(43−T_i) Δt_i is the piecewise-constant special case. It is printed as a record, never asserted: a rectangle rule cannot reach 1e-10 on a ramp.

    Anchors (asserted at 1e-10 relative — the §7 row's bar). - (A1) Three linear ramps straddling 43 °C against the analytic piecewise integral above, written out in the test as its own two-line function. The test does not call the operator for this; that independence is the point. The ramps, one per branch of the operator: - 41 → 45 °C over 600 s (rising); - 42.5 → 43.5 °C over 60 s (a shallow straddle); - 45 → 40 °C over 900 s (falling, b < 0).
