    IMP-5 — history¶

    Item briefs and execution history (operator directive 2026-10-01). The §7 row is the status of record.

    Opened by the 2026-10-03 18:00 daily review under §9 rule (5), lane 3, on a closed-form licence: the anchor is the exact Bessel/Hankel series of a dielectric-coated PEC circular cylinder. This is Phase 7 subgoal (7a), canonical conductors in a lossy host against closed forms, and the natural successor of IMP-4 (✅ 2026-10-03, the bare PEC cylinder on the hole route). The weekly ratifies or reverts the opening.

    What it adds over IMP-4. The insulated lead shaft. A lossless dielectric sleeve a < ρ < b around the PEC conductor puts the project's first conforming dielectric interface (n̂ × E, n̂ × H and n̂·D continuous) under a closed form at finite k, in the lossy host. In the TE drive the contrast across that interface is |ε_c,host/ε_c,coat| ≈ 59.

Cite Harrington, Time-Harmonic Electromagnetic Fields (1961), ch. 5 (§5-8, scattering by cylinders; the coated case is an extension of that section's method), and Balanis, Advanced Engineering Electromagnetics, ch. 11 (scattering by circular cylinders).     Closed forms (single-mode drives, e^{+jωt}, H⁽²⁾ outgoing; the docstring derives each). Regions: coating a ≤ ρ < b (k₁ = k_coat), host ρ ≥ b (k₂ = k_host). - TM_z, n = 0 (axial E). - Coating: E_z = E₀·c·g(ρ), with g = J₀(k₁ρ)Y₀(k₁a) − Y₀(k₁ρ)J₀(k₁a), so g(a) = 0. - Host: E_z = E₀[J₀(k₂ρ) + s H₀⁽²⁾(k₂ρ)]. - At ρ = b, E_z and ∂_ρE_z are continuous (μ equal, so H_φ = ∂_ρE_z/(jωμ₀) is continuous). That is a 2 × 2 solve for (c, s). - Wire current I = 2πaH_φ(a) = −4E₀c/(jωμ₀), because g′(a) = −2/(πa) by the J/Y Wronskian (E.19). - TE_z, n = 1 (transverse E). - Coating: H_z = H₀·c·h(ρ) cos φ, with h = J₁(k₁ρ)Y₁′(k₁a) − Y₁(k₁ρ)J₁′(k₁a), so h′(a) = 0 ⇔ E_φ(a) = 0. - Host: H_z = H₀[J₁(k₂ρ) + t H₁⁽²⁾(k₂ρ)] cos φ, with IMP-4's H₀ = −2jωε_c,host E₀/k₂ (incident E₀ŷ on the axis). - At ρ = b, H_z a ε_c⁻¹∂_ρH_z are continuous, with ε_c,coat/ε_c,ht = k₁²/k₂². That is a 2 × 2 solve for (c, t). - E = ∇ × (H_zẑ)/(jωε_c) uses the region's own ε_c, so in the coating E carries the factor k₂²/k₁². ω cancels out of E, as in IMP-4. - Quasi-static TE (k → 0, ratio r = k₁²/k₂² kept), derived independently. Let q = a²/b² and Δ = (1 − q) + r(1 + q). - Host: E_ρ = (E₀ + B/ρ²) sin φ and E_φ = (E₀ − B/ρ²) cos φ, with B = E₀b²[r(1 + q) − (1 − q)]/Δ. - Coating: E_ρ = −C(1 + a²/ρ²) sin φ and E_φ = −C(1 − a²/ρ²) cos φ, with C = −2E₀/Δ. - At r = 1 this is IMP-4's static field (B = E₀a²).
