Otherwise it runs stage 3 (read). The Study is built in the script with explicit arguments, not through _d2_study(): - FourPortBirdcageGeometry.f_small(); - GelledSalinePhantomMaterial(SALINE_SIGMA, SALINE_EPSILON_R, FREQUENCY_64_HZ); - LumpedPortDrive(); - frequency_hz=FREQUENCY_64_HZ, degree=2.
