    Four-port EM outputs from YAML: 10 g SAR, |B₁⁺| and point probes¶

    What you will compute¶

    The F-small four-port birdcage (exact port rectangles, κ off, degree 1, 64 MHz) around its saline phantom, driven one port at a time at 1 V. From the one field-keeping solve the file's outputs share, a study file can ask for three readings per port drive: the 10 g mass-averaged SAR in a ball at one centre per port (the {ring_radius_m: r} form puts each centre at radius r on its port's azimuth), |B₁⁺| at listed points, and point SAR and |E| at listed points inside the phantom. Each file runs through one call, fem_em_solver.run_study_file, which writes the JSON summary (and the Touchstone file of the S-matrix) next to the results.

    Before you start¶

    You need the complex DolfinX build (source /usr/local/bin/dolfinx-complex-mode) and 2 MPI ranks; each block below takes ≈ 2 min. Copy the two study files next to your script: f_small_sar_b1.yaml and f_small_em_probes.yaml.

    The study files¶

    File What it sets Summary keys it adds Record it carries
    f_small_sar_b1.yaml outputs.sar (10 g, centres on a ring) and outputs.b1_plus (listed points) sar, b1_plus WF-1 step 5 (item 344): the 10 g C4 cyclic pairs and the |B₁⁺| C4 covariance
    f_small_em_probes.yaml outputs.probes (five points in the phantom) probes WF-1 step 20 (U70): the summary is point_sar on the run's own fields

    Probe points outside the phantom are refused, because the σ and ρ that turn |E| into SAR are the phantom's. On this family probes takes {points: […]}; on the thermal files it takes a list of balls instead.

    Run them¶

    Each block runs one file and prints which port drives its summary holds.

    ```python four-port-sar-b1 from fem_em_solver import run_study_file

    run = run_study_file("f_small_sar_b1.yaml", output_dir="femem_out/f_small_sar_b1") print(sorted(run.summary["sar"]["values"])) print(sorted(run.summary["b1_plus"]["values"]))

    
```python four-port-probes
from fem_em_solver import run_study_file

run = run_study_file("f_small_em_probes.yaml", output_dir="femem_out/f_small_em_probes")
print(sorted(run.summary["probes"]["values"]))

## What you should see

The C4 symmetry of the birdcage, read from the run. The 10 g SAR at port k's
own centre under port k's drive, compared cyclically between neighbours; and
`|B₁⁺|` under ports 2, 4 and 3 on a point set rotated by +Δ, −Δ and 2Δ (Δ the
port spacing), against port 1 on the unrotated set. The test, not the block,
computes these from the run; the pairing reads the solved sheets' azimuths,
not the port names.

```text four-port-records
10 g C4 cyclic pairs (%): 0.8702 3.5284 2.0337 4.5024
|B1+| C4 covariance (%): 1.8695 1.9494 1.6405

    What this does not show¶

    These are per-1 V port-drive records, not absolute or compliance SAR or |B₁⁺|; there is no spatial-peak search and no homogeneity figure. Degree 1 only (the degree-2 record is a wider-rank record and is not run here); the coil is the solid σ = 800 S/m conductor, not copper; no F-human; no external-solver comparison.
