    Measured degree-2 clinical prices beside the estimator¶

    The estimator's degree-2 1.5 T row (FC2, ranks 16) was measured on the F-clinical-1.5T r1 mesh (no rod) and its seconds are one EM solve with the mesh build excluded; xxl entry 62 ran the rod-in-box 1.5 T mesh at degree 2 at -n 16 in two calls in one window -- call 1 C6 with the box-wide grid, call 2 I36 in two arms (tag 403 as each row states). Each call's seconds are that call's window (build, EM solves and readouts), the window's seconds the harness wall time, and the GiB the cgroup memory.peak over the whole window, so the call rows carry no GiB and no GiB ratio. The ratios therefore set two different quantities, on two meshes, at one width, beside each other. Measured values are the strings of docs/testing/xxl-ledger.md; the estimator values are the CLI's printed repr of femem run --estimate tests/package/wf13/f_clinical_15t_d2.yaml.
