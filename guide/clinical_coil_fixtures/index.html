    Clinical-scale body-coil fixtures (F-clinical 1.5 T / 3 T)¶

    This page collects the geometry of the two clinical-scale birdcage fixtures of GEO-38 (PROJECT_PLAN §10 chain C), the sources each number comes from, and the rulings that fixed the modelling choices. Every number in the geometry table below is checked against the code by tests/package/test_geo38_clinical_fixture_page.py, and every number in the heating section by tests/package/test_wf17_clinical_heating_page.py, and every number in the rod-in-box section by tests/package/test_wf17_clinical_rod_records_page.py, and every number in the closed-form gel-box section by tests/package/test_wf17_clinical_closed_form_records_page.py.

    What the two fixtures are¶

    F-clinical-1.5T (64 MHz) and F-clinical-3T (128 MHz) are nominal clinical body-coil geometries, not scanner models: 32-leg high-pass birdcages inside a closed PEC can, with the ASTM F2182 gel box as the load. F-clinical is the production fixture for new human-scale work; the Phase 6 exit stays on F-human (PROJECT_PLAN §10, the clinical-scale coil fixtures paragraph).

    Both are built by the same generator. The 3 T table is derived from the 1.5 T table by overriding five values (rung diameter, rung span, leg spacing, shield radius, air padding); everything else is inherited.

    Notes on the rows:

    • Shield (can) length is not a typed parameter. The generator places the can at ±z_extent with z_extent = max(coil_length/2, leg_spacing/2 + ring_minor_radius, phantom_height/2) + air_padding; the 3 T table computes its air_padding so that the can is the sourced 0.70 m.
    • Rung length at 3 T (review ruling (b), 2026-10-18 03:00): the sourced 0.58 m is the leg span (coil_length), asserted at 1e-9; the ring-centreline separation keeps the 1.5 T absolute leg overhang (0.0321428571 m past each ring), so it reads 0.516 m. The 1.5 T convention (sourced 0.45 m = ring-centreline separation, leg span 0.5143 m) is not carried over.
    • Box cells. With the ASTM box in place: 1.5 T 1 149 150 cells at -n 1 / 2 / 4 (C2, 20261015T185056Z_GEO-38-s2-W3-n4.log); 3 T 1 061 517 cells at -n 1 / 2 / 4 (C8b -n 1, 20261019T172435Z_GEO-38-C8b-W1-box-n1.log; C8b -n 2, 20261019T173044Z_GEO-38-C8b-W2-box-n2.log; C8 -n 4, 20261018T171406Z_GEO-38-C8-W3-box-n4.log).
    • Can-cap clearance at 3 T (review ruling (c)): the box ends sit 25 mm from the can caps; the closed can stays, and the clearance is named for the weekly.

    Sources¶

    The three sources found 2026-10-14 (docs/planning/chunks/GEO-38.md, "Nominal geometry"):

    • https://mri-q.com/uploads/3/4/5/7/34572113/body_coil_distriubtion_mrm.20177.pdf — "Efficient high-frequency body coil for high-field MRI" (MRM): the 3 T high-pass quadrature body coil, 58 cm long, 70.4 cm in diameter, inside a shield 74.3 cm in diameter and 70 cm long. Supplies the 3 T rung diameter, shield diameter, shield length and rung length.
    • https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12137786/ — a 70 cm-bore 3 T scanner modelled with a 32-rung high-pass birdcage. Supplies the leg count (32) and the high-pass topology.
    • https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/11442125 — US patent 11 442 125: 1.5 T rungs 45 cm long on a carrier of 65 cm inner diameter, the RF shield within ≈ 2 cm of the end-ring diameter. Supplies the 1.5 T rung length and the shield-to-ring spacing.

    Modelling choices, not sourced:

    • the 1.5 T rung diameter 0.710 m, set just outside the 0.70 m patient space rather than at the patent's 65 cm carrier;
    • the 1.5 T shield length, which follows from the air-padding rule (padding = (0.03/0.07) · ring_radius) rather than from a source;
    • the leg overhang past each ring (0.0321428571 m);
    • the leg-box section (4 × 4 × 21.43 mm);
    • the closed PEC can itself (a modelling stand-in for the RF shield);
    • the 3 T rung-length reading (0.58 m as the leg span, ruling (b)).

    The weekly may refine a value only with a cited source (PROJECT_PLAN §10).

    The rulings¶

    • The human operator's port-census ruling (2026-10-15): 32 legs ⇒ every ring gap of both rings is a lumped sheet, 64 sheets and a 64×64 untuned S-matrix; tuning and SAR by two ports in quadrature with the 64 gaps as series-RC sheets at the 0.2 Ω ESR; 16 legs considered and rejected because it contradicts the cited 32-rung source.
    • The 2026-10-15 03:00 review's provisional choices: rung length = the ring-centreline separation; the shield as a closed PEC can (new keyword-only shield_radius); F-human's absolute conductor sizing; C1's load a volume-matched gel cylinder; the leg-box dy cut to 4 mm.
    • The 2026-10-15 10:30 corner ruling: leg-box dx 4 mm; the design corner clearance ≥ 5 mm; the shield_radius refusal computes the leg-box corner radius over the actual box azimuths; shield_radius is refused together with leg_gap_length.
    • All of the above ratified by the 2026-10-17 weekly.
    • The 2026-10-15 18:00 ruling concerns the build cache for the C1 (b) column (-n 4 build, column alone at -n 8), not geometry.
    • Item 518's rulings (2026-10-18 03:00 review): (b) the 3 T rung length is the weekly's 0.58 m, asserted at 1e-9 on the rung (leg) span, the chosen parameter (coil_length) printed; (c) the closed PEC can stays and the 25 mm cap-to-box clearance is printed and named for the weekly.

    How it is built¶

    The 1.5 T table is the single source of truth; the 3 T table is a derived dict. Both are passed to MeshGenerator.birdcage_port_domain, whose keyword-only shield_radius adds the closed PEC can and whose phantom_box places the ASTM box as tag 3.

    F_CLINICAL_15T_KWARGS   tests/validation/test_geo38_f_clinical_build.py
F_CLINICAL_3T_KWARGS    tests/validation/test_geo38_f_clinical_3t.py
MeshGenerator.birdcage_port_domain(..., shield_radius=..., phantom_box=...)
gate modules            tests/validation/test_geo38_f_clinical_build.py   (C1 (a))
                        tests/validation/test_geo38_f_clinical_box.py     (C2)
                        tests/validation/test_geo38_f_clinical_3t.py      (C8)

    The meshing example that builds the 1.5 T fixture with the box is the mesh:15 guide.

    The two ratio rows are the 3 T / 1.5 T quotient of θ(t*) at each field's own c*, as GEO-38 C39 printed them in 20261111T103024Z_GEO-38-C39-W1.log.

    • degree 1. Both records are degree-1 fields; no degree-2 or h-converged heating figure exists at clinical scale.
    • 1.5 T gel constants. The 3 T window uses the 1.5 T values of σ, ε_r and the thermal constants (provisional); the ratio is not a measured frequency dependence of the gel.
    • 20 °C baseline. The window also prints a cem43 per accepted watt; it is the unheated 20 °C baseline, not a dose per watt (a thermal dose is nonlinear in power), so no cem43 figure is quoted here.
    • No container wall. The box is unwalled; the container-wall thermal model is not in these records.
    • No compliance claim. These are internal records with no external check; nothing here is an ASTM F2182 or safety statement.

    • Closed form. No FEM field enters either record.

    • Per deposited watt. Both records are per deposited watt of the ball or lattice source, never per accepted watt; P_dep, P_box and P_acc are not mixed.
    • 1.5 T gel constants. Both records use the 1.5 T gel values.
    • No dose per watt. Nothing here is a compliance figure, a dose band, a damage threshold or a tissue model; 100 W is not a realistic drive.
    • A record. Provisional: a planning review reads the verdict, and no band is set here.

    Not claimed¶

    Beyond the heating records and the rod-in-box records (internal, degree 1) and the closed-form records (internal, closed form) above: no port gate, S-matrix, tuning, resonance, B₁⁺ or SAR figure is quoted on this page; not a scanner model; no ASTM compliance claim; the closed can is not claimed to be the physical shield.

    Erratum¶

    Resolved. The mesh:15 guide (examples/meshing/15_f_clinical_coil_astm_box.md) gave the can as "length 0.745 m", which was its diameter. §9 item 543 (an EX-117 follow-on) corrected it to 0.954285714 m (2·z_extent, 20261015T171259Z_GEO-38-s1a-W2-r2-a2.log), together with the stale "pending the weekly's ratification" text for choices the 2026-10-17 weekly had ratified. The table above always carried the right value.
